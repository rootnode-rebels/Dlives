# Privacy Policy for Dlives
Last updated: September 4, 2026

Dlives is designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

## Data Collection
Dlives does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Dlives has no backend, no analytics, and no telemetry. All application data is stored exclusively on your local device.

## Local Data Storage
All data created or used within Dlives — including alarms, timetable entries, notes, calendar events, tasks, settings, and clipboard/shelf history — is stored locally on your device at `%APPDATA%\Dlives\`. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere by the application.

## System Permissions
Dlives requests the following Windows permissions, used only as described:

- **Notification access**: used to display a local, in-app summary of your system notifications for your convenience. Notification content is processed and displayed entirely on your device and is never transmitted, logged externally, or shared.
- **Media session access**: used to display "Now Playing" information (track title, artist, artwork) and allow playback control (play/pause/skip) for media playing elsewhere on your system. This information is read locally and never transmitted anywhere.
- **Exact alarm scheduling**: used to reliably trigger your alarms and timetable reminders at the scheduled time.

## Third-Party Services
Dlives does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party service, advertising network, or analytics platform.
